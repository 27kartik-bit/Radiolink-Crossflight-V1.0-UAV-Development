# Electronic Speed Controllers (ESCs) and Brushless Motors

## 1. Overview

This section documents the propulsion system used in the Radiolink Crossflight V1.0 quadcopter.

The propulsion system consists of brushless DC motors and Electronic Speed Controllers (ESCs), which work together to generate and regulate the thrust required for flight.

## 2. Electronic Speed Controllers

**Component:** SimonK 30A ESCs

### Function

Electronic Speed Controllers regulate the speed of brushless motors by controlling the electrical power supplied to them.

### Technical Role

* Motor speed regulation.
* Communication between the flight control system and propulsion system.
* Power delivery to individual motors.
* Propulsion system integration.

## 3. Brushless DC Motors

### Function

Brushless DC motors generate the rotational force required to drive the propellers and produce thrust.

### Technical Role

* Conversion of electrical energy into mechanical rotation.
* Generation of thrust through propeller rotation.
* Integration with ESCs for motor speed control.

## 4. System Integration

The ESCs were integrated with the brushless motors and flight control system as part of the quadcopter propulsion architecture.
