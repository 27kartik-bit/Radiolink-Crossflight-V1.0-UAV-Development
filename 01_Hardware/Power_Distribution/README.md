## 2. LiPo Battery

**Battery Type:** Lithium Polymer (LiPo)  
**Cell Configuration:** 4S

### Function
The LiPo battery serves as the primary electrical
energy source for the quadcopter.

### Technical Specifications
- Battery chemistry: Lithium Polymer
- Cell configuration: 4S
- Nominal voltage: 14.8V
- Fully charged voltage: 16.8V

### Technical Role
- Supplies electrical power to the propulsion system.
- Provides energy for onboard electronics.
- Supports the electrical requirements of the UAV.

**Battery capacity:** To be verified.

## 3. Power Distribution Board

### Function
The Power Distribution Board distributes electrical
power from the battery to connected components.

### Technical Role
- Power distribution to ESCs.
- Electrical connectivity between battery and propulsion system.
- Organisation of onboard power architecture.
